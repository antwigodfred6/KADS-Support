# KADS Support: Proposed Sitemap & URL Plan

Status: **approved 11 Nov 2026: Option B (clean URLs + 301s) implemented.**

## Principle
Keep every existing URL that has real content (they have been indexed since 2018). Only change a URL when the old one is actively misleading. Every changed or removed URL gets a **301 redirect** in `next.config.ts`.

## Option A: keep all content URLs (recommended for launch)

Lowest SEO risk and no redirects needed for content pages. Only the nav labels change.

| Page | URL | Nav label | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Home | `/` | (logo) | `/` |
| Accommodation | `/our-services/` | Accommodation | `/our-services/` |
| Employment & Training | `/employment-training/` | Employment & Training | `/employment-training/` |
| Advice & Support | `/support-programs/` | Advice & Support | `/support-programs/` |
| Our Objectives | `/our-objectives/` | Our Objectives | `/our-objectives/` |
| About us | `/about-dacs/` | About us | `/about-dacs/` |
| Contact | `/contact/` | Contact | `/contact/` |

## Option B: clean URLs with 301s

Clearer for users and search engines (e.g. `/accommodation` actually says accommodation). The cost is a short ranking wobble while Google processes the redirects. For a small site with low current traffic, I think B is worth it, but it's your call.

| Page | New URL | 301 from |
|---|---|---|
| Home | `/` | `/sample-page/`, `/blog/`, `/shop/`, `/basket/`, `/checkout/`, `/my-account/` |
| Accommodation | `/accommodation` | `/our-services/` |
| Employment & Training | `/employment-training` | (unchanged) |
| Advice & Support | `/advice-support` | `/support-programs/` |
| Our Objectives | `/our-objectives` | (unchanged) |
| About us | `/about` | `/about-dacs/` (and `/about` already redirects the other way today) |
| Contact | `/contact` | (unchanged) |

Trailing slashes: the current site uses them (`/contact/`). With Option A I'll set `trailingSlash: true` so URLs match exactly; with Option B, no trailing slash, and Next.js will redirect the slash versions automatically.

## Removed pages (both options): 301 → `/`
`/sample-page/` (WordPress placeholder) · `/blog/` (empty) · `/shop/` · `/basket/` · `/checkout/` · `/my-account/` (unused WooCommerce)

**Header (mobile):** logo + "Call" icon button + menu drawer (same links, plus phone and email at the bottom)

**Footer:**

## Sitemap tree

```
/
├── /our-services/  or /accommodation
├── /employment-training/
├── /support-programs/ or /advice-support
├── /our-objectives/
├── /about-dacs/ or /about
├── /referrals            (new)
├── /contact/
├── /privacy-policy       (new, awaiting content)
├── /cookie-policy        (new, awaiting content)
└── /accessibility        (new, draft for approval)
```

`sitemap.xml` and `robots.txt` are generated by Next.js. Pages awaiting content are set to `noindex` until approved.
